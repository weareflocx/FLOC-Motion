FLOC Motion adapts the original SVG geometry and animation fields to local
Canvas2D backgrounds. Pointer input is replaced by a seeded, time-derived
ambient trajectory, and animation state is reconstructed deterministically
for preview, seeking and video export. No Pro textures are included.
